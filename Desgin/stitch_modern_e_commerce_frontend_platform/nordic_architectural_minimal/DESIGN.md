## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, commercial modernist aesthetic at the intersection of high-end editorial commerce and purposeful functional utility. Drawing direct inspiration from Scandinavian restraint, mid-century industrial discipline, and contemporary digital flagship ateliers, the atmosphere evokes architectural clarity, quiet confidence, and frictionless luxury.

The target audience comprises discerning modern consumers and design-conscious professionals seeking timeless essentials across living, hardware, and tech. The emotional response is centered on calm, decisive clarity: the UI recedes deliberately into the background, providing an unadorned gallery stage where products, typography, and tactile photography take precedence. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an elevated Minimalist movement infused with low-contrast structural outlines and subtle tonal layering. Interaction patterns avoid ornamental fluff; micro-interactions are crisp, swift, and calibrated with subtle dampening to communicate craftsmanship, precision, and reliable performance.

## Colors

The palette establishes a warm, gallery-grade foundation that reduces visual fatigue while maximizing product contrast. 

- **Primary Canvas & Backgrounds (`#FAF9F6`)**: A soft alabaster off-white providing warmth without muddy undertones, eliminating the sterile harshness of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Ink & Key Affordances (`#111827`)**: Deep obsidian charcoal serving as dominant typography, high-impact CTA containers, and authoritative structural framing.
- **Secondary / Utility Accent (`#059669`)**: A balanced, saturated pine emerald reserved strictly for optimistic states: in-stock flags, positive sustainability ratings, promo badges, and price differentials.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#4F46E5`)**: A precise digital indigo used sparingly for focus rings, interactive states, digital product highlights, and active filter tabs.
- **Neutral Boundaries & Surfaces**: Component containers rely on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) against the off-white canvas, framed by refined structural strokes (`#E5E7EB` to `#F3F4F6`). Muted body typography utilizes slate grey (`#4B5563` and `#6B7280`) ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility across light backgrounds.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic tiers, unifying display, narrative, and operational UI with geometric balance and contemporary clarity.

- **Headlines**: Tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) ensures tight, editorial authority at larger display sizes, preventing loose words and establishing clear hierarchy.
- **Body Text**: Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure scanning clarity on product spec sheets, customer reviews, and editorial long-form features.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Form labels, category slugs, and inventory badges adopt slight positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) with semibold or bold weights, maintaining supreme legibility across dense e-commerce checkouts and filter bars.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column system, bounded by a maximum viewport content constraint of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with a `1.5rem` gutter and `3rem` side margin. High-density gallery listings default to 3 or 4 columns; hero editorial spreads take 6 or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with a `1.25rem` gutter and `2rem` outer margin. Dual-column product arrangements dominate listings.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns with a `1rem` gutter and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Grids gracefully collapse to single or dual-item staggered feeds.

Structural rhythm relies strictly on the `space-*` tokens for stack layouts, input heights, and card containers. Sections alternate with ample vertical whitespace (`space-xl` scaled across multiples) to echo museum-like negative space and avoid clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast outlines** and **ambient shadows** to produce a clean physical presence that mirrors premium industrial design packaging.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Neutral surface (`#FAF9F6`) without borders or shadows.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Listings)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed by a 1px solid stroke in `#E5E7EB`. Under ambient light, a whisper-soft shadow is applied: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Hover & Modals)**: Subtle physical lift for interactive product cards and quick-view triggers: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`. Border changes softly to `#D1D5DB`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Popovers & Slide-out Cart Drawer)**: Frosted translucency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-blur) paired with a defined lower edge `1px solid rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.8)` and directional shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances modern ergonomics with architectural precision:

- **Standard Containers (`0.5rem` / `rounded`)**: Product thumbnail containers, standard input fields, alert banners, and checkout modules.
- **Larger Components (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`)**: Primary hero banners, product detail zoom panels, flyout panels, and modal shells.
- **Structural Framing (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`)**: Architectural section callouts, collection highlights, and feature banners.
- **Pills / Radiused Caps**: Status pills, interactive filter chips, and circular icon utility buttons use fully rounded pill geometry to distinguish interactive chips from square product imagery.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary Action*: High-density solid `#111827` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. On hover, background shifts to `#1F2937` with an active micro-scale transition (`scale(0.99)`).
  - *Secondary / Ghost Action*: Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border, `#111827` text. On hover, border shifts to `#111827`.
  - *Accent Purchase*: Reserved for immediate "Buy Now" or promo flows, utilizing `#059669` fill with pure white typography.
  - *Focus State*: `0 0 0 2px #FAF9F6, 0 0 0 4px #4F46E5`.

- **Product Cards**:
  - Composed of a pure `#FFFFFF` shell, 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), and `0.5rem` border radius.
  - Image frame utilizes an off-white sub-canvas (`#F3F4F6`) with `aspect-ratio: 4/5` and `object-fit: cover`.
  - Badges sit pinned top-left with `0.5rem` inset margin.
  - Wishlist icon buttons float top-right on a semi-translucent circular disc (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).

- **Chips & Filter Pills**:
  - Default: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, `#4B5563` text, pill radius (`9999px`), `0.375rem 0.875rem` padding.
  - Selected: `#111827` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, borderless.

- **Form Inputs & Search Field**:
  - Height: `44px` minimum for tap ergonomics.
  - Inactive: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E5E7EB` border, `#111827` value text, `#9CA3AF` placeholder.
  - Focused: Background remains `#FFFFFF`, stroke shifts to `#111827` with 1px outer ring in `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.

- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Checkbox: `18px` square with `0.25rem` radius. Border 1.5px solid `#D1D5DB`. Active fill `#111827` with crisp white SVG check.
  - Radio: `18px` circle with concentric center disc on active selection (`#111827`).

- **Badges & Micro-Labels**:
  - In-Stock / Sustainability: `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` typography, `label-badge` styling.
  - Low Stock / Limited Release: `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` typography.
  - Editorial / Category Flag: `#F3F4F6` background, `#374151` typography.

- **Mini-Cart / Slide-Out Drawer**:
  - Anchored right, `440px` desktop width, 100% viewport height, frosted backdrop scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)`).
  - Divided by clean `1px solid #F3F4F6` lines between item rows with instant quantity modifier increments.
